Drop your PC photo here as:

    case.png   (or case.jpg — then set the <img src> in index.html accordingly)

The app shows this photo in the "Case Layout & Airflow" panel with the intake/
exhaust arrows overlaid on top. If the file is missing, the app draws a
built-in schematic of the case instead (same fan positions and airflow).

Recommended: a side-view / glass-panel shot of the case, roughly 4:3, so the
three top fans, three front fans and the rear fan line up with the arrows.
